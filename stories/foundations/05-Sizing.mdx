# Sizing

<div className="lede">
How big things are, on the same 4px grid as spacing — <code>--size-10</code>
is 40px exactly as <code>--space-10</code> is 40px. Control heights are
floors, applied as <code>min-height</code>, so a control can always grow when
its label wraps or its text is scaled up.
</div>

---

## 1. The Scale

<SizeScale />

**36px and 72px were dropped** from the scale this started as. Each sat
between two neighbours with no job: a control is either compact (32) or
default (40), never 36. Every step that survived is used by at least one
semantic token — a step nothing points at is a step somebody will misuse.

---

## 2. Controls

Five heights. Buttons, inputs, selects and icon buttons all read the same
token, so they line up when placed side by side.

**`min-height`, never `height`.** A fixed height clips a wrapped label and
clips text that someone has scaled up. The floor sets the rhythm; content is
allowed to win.

## 3. Icons & Avatars

## 4. Touch Targets

<TouchTargets />

WCAG 2.2 **SC 2.5.8 (AA) requires 24×24 CSS px**; SC 2.5.5 (AAA) asks for
44×44. This is a product people book trips on from a phone, so **44px is the
working minimum** and 24px is the floor nothing may go below.

The visible control and its hit area are different things. A 24px icon can
carry a 44px target by growing its padding — that is nearly always the right
fix, and it is why `--control-height-md` grows from 40 to 44 below 640px.

  ['modal-height-max','90vh',                   'Never taller than the screen.'],
]} />

**Fixed, flexible, content-driven** are three different things. Headers and
sidebars are fixed. Modals and drawers are flexible — the token is a ceiling
and the panel is as small as its content allows. Cards and list rows are
content-driven and get no height token at all.

---

## 6. Aspect Ratios

<RatioGrid />

Ratio boxes crop with `object-fit: cover`, never distort. Use one whenever
images sit in a grid, so a portrait photo cannot make its card taller than
its neighbours.

## 7. Width & Limits

<TokenTable rows={[

  ['button-width-min','64px',            'So a two-letter label is not a dot.'],
  ['input-width-max', '480px',           'Wider is harder to scan, not easier.'],
  ['content-width-max','65ch',           'From typography. Caps prose.'],
  ['card-width-min',  '260px',           'Below this a card stops being one.'],
]} />

**Do not create a fixed width where `fit-content` or a max would do.** Fixed
widths are the first thing to break when a label is translated — German runs
roughly 35% longer than English, and Hindi sets taller.

---

## 8. Responsive

| | Mobile ≤640 | Tablet ≤1024 | Desktop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control md | **44px** | 40px | 40px |
| Control sm | **40px** | 32px | 32px |
| Header | 56px | 64px | 64px |
| Drawer / modal | full width | fixed | fixed |
| Sidebar | drawer | collapsed 64px | 280px |
| Icons, avatars | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged |

**Controls grow on small screens, they do not shrink.** The finger is the
same size whatever the screen is. Icons and avatars stay put — they are
already sized against text, which does not shrink either.
