Seven families, eleven steps each, generated in OKLCH. Two brand values are

### Status

Four kinds, four slots each: content, surface, border, icon.

<StatusBanner kind="success">Your trip to Udaipur is confirmed.</StatusBanner>
<StatusBanner kind="warning">Only 2 rooms left at this price.</StatusBanner>
<StatusBanner kind="error">We could not read that date. Try DD/MM/YYYY.</StatusBanner>
<StatusBanner kind="info">Visa on arrival is available for this route.</StatusBanner>

Always pair a status colour with an icon and words. Colour alone is invisible
to a colour-blind reader — and the brand colour is already a red.

## Alpha

<TokenTable rows={[
  ['alpha-hairline', '8%',  'Weak borders, hover wash.'],
  ['alpha-weak',     '16%', 'Medium borders, pressed wash.'],
  ['alpha-medium',   '32%', 'Disabled overlays, heavy dividers.'],
  ['alpha-strong',   '56%', 'Modal scrim, image scrim.'],
  ['alpha-solid',    '72%', 'Heavy scrim over busy photography.'],
]} />

Mixed from `--alpha-base`, which is `neutral-900` in light and `neutral-50` in
dark. Flipping that one value is what makes the border tokens work in both
themes without being rewritten.
